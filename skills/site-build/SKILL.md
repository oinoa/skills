# Site build

A site has one job: the visitor does one thing (calls, leaves a request, buys). Every block
on the page serves that action or goes. Build one working piece at a time, and show the
page itself, never a description of it.

## Procedure

1. **The brief, in one message.** What the visitor should do, who they are, what is sold
   and at what price, the contacts, and what already exists: a domain, a site or shop
   platform, a logo, photos, a price list. Ask only for what blocks the first version.
2. **Use what they have.** A site already on a platform (WordPress, Tilda and the like) is
   changed there. A new landing with nothing behind it is one self-contained HTML file:
   styles inside, no build step, made for a phone first. A new domain, hosting or any
   paid service is suggested with its price, never bought.
3. **Write the page in this order:** the offer and the action in the first screen, the
   proof (what is included, photos, real reviews only), the price or how it is formed, the
   answers to the usual questions, the action again, the contacts. Missing facts are a
   marked gap `[цена?]`, never a guess. No invented reviews, numbers or awards.
4. **Products from a table.** Take the person's list or price sheet. One row per product:
   title, price, description, photo file, category, stock. Write the cards with
   **shop-desk**. Load them through the platform's import (a CSV) or its admin; check five
   cards by eye after loading.
5. **Check before you show it.**
   - Phone width (360 px) and a computer: nothing cut off, text readable, buttons big
     enough to tap.
   - Every link and button goes where it says. The form is sent once and arrives where the
     person reads it.
   - Phone numbers, addresses, prices and dates match what the person gave.
   - Images are compressed; the page opens fast on mobile data.
   - The page has a title and a short description for search.
   - A form that collects names or phones needs a consent line and a privacy policy page.
6. **Show it.** A preview (a screenshot or the file) and one line on what you checked and
   what you did not. Then ask: publish?
7. **Publish only after a clear yes** to this version. Afterwards open the live page once
   and say it is live, with the link.

## Edits from a voice message

Change exactly what was asked and nothing else. Repeat the change back in one line before you
publish it ("меняю цену тура на 45 000 на главной и в карточке, больше ничего"). Show the
before and after.

## Rules

- Publishing, deleting a page or a product, changing a live price, giving anyone access and
  any payment wait for the person's clear yes on that item.
- Never ask for a password or a key in the chat. If the work needs their site's admin
  access, say exactly which access is needed and let the person decide how to give it.
- Keep a copy of the page or the product list before you change it, so a change can be undone.

## Worked example

The person: "Нужен лендинг на осенний тур, заявки пусть падают мне на почту." They sent the
programme, the price and three photos.

Reply in chat after the first version:

```
Лендинг готов, скриншоты с телефона и компьютера ниже. Форма заявки отправляет письмо на вашу почту, я проверил одной тестовой заявкой.
Не хватает одного: сколько мест в группе. Пока на странице [мест?].
Публикуем после этого?
```

What was checked behind that message: the first screen shows the tour, the dates, the price
and the "Оставить заявку" button at 360 px; all three photos are compressed; the test request
arrived; the phone number in the footer matches the one in the brief. Nothing is live until
the person answers yes.
